Hand-drawn NerdWorkbench icons (MIT, see assets/fonts/nerdworkbench/LICENSE-ICONS).

Grid: 11 columns x 8 rows of Topaz bricks (1.5 x 2 px at 16 px), '#' = ink.
A brick is taller than wide: a round shape is ~8 columns by 6 rows.
Each entry: "U+XXXX name [alias U+YYYY ...]" then 8 rows. Aliases share the
drawing so one meaning always has one picture (e.g. MDI and Font Awesome
volume icons). Batteries are generated in tools/build-fonts.py.
